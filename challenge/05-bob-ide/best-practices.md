# Bob Best Practices

Follow the best practices guidelines to get the most out of IBM Bob:

https://bob.ibm.com/docs/ide/getting-started/best-practices

## Quick tips from the hackathon guide

- **Use the hackathon-provisioned Bob account** to avoid consuming personal Bobcoins.
- **Divide tasks among teammates** to make full use of the 40 Bobcoin allocation.
- Use **Agent mode, parallel tasks, subagents, and document understanding** to manage multiple steps.
- Follow best practices to use Bob **effectively and efficiently** (important because Bobcoin budget is limited).
