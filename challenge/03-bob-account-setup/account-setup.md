# Set Up Your IBM Bob Account

## To access the hackathon-provisioned IBM Bob account

- Participants **must be registered** for the hackathon.
- If you already have personal Bob access, **use the hackathon-provisioned Bob account** for this event to avoid consuming usage on your personal account.

## Confirm your hackathon IBM Bob account

At the start, all registered members receive an **email invite** to join the hackathon-provisioned Bob account.

Steps:
1. Check the email inbox you registered with; open the invite from the IBM Bob team.
   - Confirm you have the correct email by looking for: **"You have been added as a team member to ibm-hackathon-xxxx"**
   - **Note**: This team-member reference is just enablement terminology for an Enterprise account — it does NOT mean you're assigned to a team. Teams are formed via the supported hackathon platforms.
   - **Bob Plan**: Enterprise plan
   - Check **Junk/Spam** folders if not found; search for "IBM Bob".
2. **Create an IBMid** (if you don't have one). IBMid is required to access Bob.
   - IBMid account: https://www.ibm.com/docs/en/storage-defender/base?topic=in-creating-ibmid
   - IBMid sign-up for hackathon email: https://www.ibm.com/account/reg/us-en/signup?formid=urx-19776

## Sign into Bob IDE

- Open Bob IDE and select **Log in to Bob**.
- Complete IBMid authentication in your browser.
- **Important**: If you have multiple Bob accounts, select the **hackathon-provisioned instance named `ibm-coding-challenge-uat (region: us-east)`** to avoid consuming Bobcoins from your personal account. Switch via Settings tab → General → team.
