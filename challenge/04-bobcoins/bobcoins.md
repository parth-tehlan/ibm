# Bobcoins — AI Usage Budget

## What are Bobcoins?

Each interaction with Bob's AI capabilities **consumes Bobcoins**.

## Hackathon budget

- **40 Bobcoins** are automatically applied to the hackathon-provisioned IBM Bob account.
- Intended to be **sufficient** for design + a compelling proof-of-concept submission.
- **Plan ahead with teammates** and divide tasks to make full use of the total allocation across all members.

## Limits

- Once usage reaches **100%**, **no additional Bobcoins will be provided** for the hackathon.
- You may **continue working** on your solution and final submission after that.
- Consider leveraging the **optional watsonx technologies** enabled for the hackathon.

## Monitoring usage

- In **Bob IDE**: select the **Settings** icon → **Settings** tab.
- Under **General**, view your Bobcoin usage.
- If you have access to multiple accounts, select the hackathon-provisioned instance named **`ibm-coding-challenge-xxx (region: us-east)`**.
- Admin dashboard: https://bob.ibm.com/admin/subscription

## Best practices

- Follow Bob best practices to use the tool effectively and efficiently (see `05-bob-ide/best-practices.md` and links in `references/`).
